Privacy Policy for Medix Doctor App

Application Name: Medix Doctor App (HMS Doctor) • Package Name: com.hms.doctor • Entity: Ariyan Hospital Multispeciality • Last Updated: August 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ariyan Hospital Multispeciality collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal, medical, and financial data through the Medix Doctor App on Android and connected web platforms.

1. Scope & Target Audience

The Medix Doctor Application is an enterprise clinical portal designed exclusively for verified healthcare practitioners, physicians, consultant doctors, and hospital administrative staff. It is not intended for use by children under the age of 18.

2. User Data We Collect

To provide electronic health records management, clinical prescriptions, and inter-hospital referrals, we collect the following categories of data:

  • Personal Practitioner Information: Doctor name, medical council registration number (NMC/State Council), specialty, qualifications, official phone number, email address, and profile photo/avatar.
  • Health & Patient Clinical Records: Patient UHID (Unique Hospital Identification Number), consultation notes, vital signs, provisional/final diagnoses, medication dosage prescriptions, and clinical referral handover summaries.
  • Financial Information: Bank account numbers, IFSC codes, and UPI Virtual Payment Addresses (VPA) provided by registered medical practitioners exclusively for settlement of OPD consultancy fees and institutional facilitation bonuses.
  • Technical & Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens required strictly for real-time delivery of emergency patient transfer alerts and lab biomarker notifications.

3. Purpose & Use of Collected Data

All data processed by the Medix Doctor App is used strictly for medical, clinical, and administrative purposes:

  • Facilitating secure inter-hospital patient admissions and bed booking triage.
  • Generating digitally authenticated electronic prescription slips (Rx).
  • Verifying practitioner medical credentials and council registration status.
  • Delivering critical laboratory diagnostic results and patient status telemetry.
  • Processing authorized practitioner fee disbursements through verified banking rails.

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade practitioner or patient medical data to any advertising networks, commercial third parties, or data brokers under any circumstances.

4. Data Storage, Security & Encryption

We implement rigorous security standards to safeguard clinical data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the Medix Doctor App and central hospital servers are transmitted over secure HTTPS using 256-bit TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Encryption at Rest: Cloud data is stored in Google Cloud Firebase Firestore with AES-256 server-side encryption.
  • Access Control: Patient records are protected by strict role-based access control (RBAC), accessible only by authorized attending medical personnel.

5. App Permissions & Disclosures

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to synchronize clinical records and hospital bed occupancy with the secure cloud database.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Required to alert doctors about incoming critical code-red patient transfers and urgent lab telemetry.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC: Optional hardware biometric fingerprint or face unlock for secure and instant practitioner authentication.
  • CAMERA / STORAGE (Optional): Used solely if the practitioner chooses to capture or upload a profile picture or scan a medical registration certificate.

6. User Rights, Data Retention & Account Deletion

Registered users have full control over their personal data. You may request:

  • Access to all personal information stored in your account.
  • Correction or updating of inaccurate profile details.
  • Permanent deletion of your practitioner profile and personal account.

To request permanent account or data deletion, email our administrative team at varshahealth01@gmail.com with the subject line “Account Deletion Request”, including your registered Doctor Name and Council Registration Number. Requests are processed within 48 business hours.

7. Compliance with Healthcare Regulations

This application complies with applicable national and international healthcare data privacy standards, including the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, HIPAA security principles, and National Medical Commission guidelines.
