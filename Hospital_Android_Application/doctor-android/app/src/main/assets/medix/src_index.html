Terms of Service & Privacy Policy

Google Play & Clinical Healthcare Compliance (DPDP Act & HIPAA standard)

1. Medical Practitioner Platform & Referral System

The Medix Clinical Referral Platform connects certified medical doctors and verified healthcare institutions. All referral requests are encrypted and dispatched directly to the chosen hospital's clinical admissions and triage department.

2. Patient Health Data Privacy & Confidentiality

Patient identifiable information (UHID, diagnosis, vitals, contact details) is handled in strict compliance with applicable Digital Personal Data Protection laws. Data is only accessible by the referring practitioner and the receiving hospital reception/triage desk.

3. Clinical Referral Bonus & Payout Terms

Referral incentives and clinical facilitation bonuses are credited upon confirmed institutional patient admission. Payouts are executed via standard IMPS/UPI banking channels to the registered practitioner account.

4. Medical Responsibility Disclaimer

This mobile application facilitates hospital transfers, scheduling, and patient routing. Clinical triage, patient management, and therapeutic interventions remain under the sole professional jurisdiction of the attending receiving hospital physicians.
