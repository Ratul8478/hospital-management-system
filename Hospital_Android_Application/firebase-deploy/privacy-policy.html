Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Last Updated: August 25, 2026

1. Medical Practitioner Platform & Marketing System

The Medix Clinical Marketing Platform connects certified medical doctors and verified healthcare institutions. All marketing requests are encrypted and dispatched directly to the chosen hospital's clinical admissions and triage department.

2. Patient Health Data Privacy & Confidentiality

Patient identifiable information (UHID, diagnosis, vitals, contact details) is handled in strict compliance with applicable Digital Personal Data Protection laws. Data is only accessible by the marketing practitioner and the receiving hospital reception/triage desk. We do not sell or share patient data with unauthorized third parties.

3. Clinical Marketing Bonus & Payout Terms

Marketing incentives and clinical facilitation bonuses are credited upon confirmed institutional patient admission. Payouts are executed via standard IMPS/UPI banking channels to the registered practitioner account.

4. Application Permissions

To provide core services, the Medix Doctor App requires access to:

  • Camera & Storage: To upload practitioner profile photos and verify professional credentials (medical licenses).
  • Network Access: To sync marketing data and hospital updates securely with our servers.

5. Data Deletion & Account Management

Practitioners may request the deletion of their account and associated data at any time by contacting our support team or using the in-app account deletion options where available.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact our administrative team.
